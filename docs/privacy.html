CaloTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

CaloTrack is a free, open calorie and fitness tracker. It is built to respect your privacy completely: all of your data stays on your own device. There is no account, no CaloTrack server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What data CaloTrack stores

Everything you enter — food entries, your food library, weight, goals, workouts, routines, and settings — is stored locally in your browser's storage on the device you use. It is never transmitted to us, because there is no “us” server to transmit it to.

WHOOP (optional)

If you choose to connect WHOOP, CaloTrack uses WHOOP's official API to import your workouts. Authentication happens through a small proxy (a Cloudflare Worker) that you deploy and control; it holds only your WHOOP application secret and stores no data. Your WHOOP access tokens are kept on your device. Imported workout data (such as calories burned and sport type) is stored locally alongside your other data and is not shared with anyone.

Open Food Facts

When you scan or enter a barcode, CaloTrack looks it up in the free public Open Food Facts database. Only the barcode number is sent to their service to retrieve product information. See their own privacy practices on their website.

Backups

You can export your data to a JSON file at any time. Those files are created by and remain under your control; CaloTrack does not upload them anywhere.

Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you can remove it entirely at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app from your home screen.
